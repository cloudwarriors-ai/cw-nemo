# Intern Management

> DoD SkillBridge Program, Vanderbilt University, Other Interns

## Purpose

Intern management under QA is designed to simultaneously:

* Protect production quality
* Develop early technical talent
* Evaluate readiness for future CSD roles

QA provides structure, mentorship, and objective evaluation while allowing interns to demonstrate initiative and problem-solving ability.

## Onboarding / Orientation

* Set-up meeting with Intern for their start day at **0930 EST**
  * Intended for this time to make sure they can attend the Daily DevOps Meeting at 1030 EST
  * Kindra, Doug, Chad, Trent invited for brief introduction if they would like to attend (optional)
* Meet with student to introduce them to CW and provide an introduction, orientation, basic information and expectations moving forward

## Application Orientation

* Conduct a BASIC walkthrough of applications for they can test
* Sometimes this is done as a checklist in their onboarding package
* **Careful always in this process to not imply issues so the student can find them naturally through using the application**

## Application Testing

* Each student is expected to follow onboarding checklist testing guidance (provides step by step guidance on how to test)
* This allows student to familiarize themselves with CW applications overall to better understand its purpose and functionality
* This provides an orientation to the application and the GitHub Repository to ensure they can effectively work on the application moving forward as a developer
* **This process shows A LOT about their character, work ethic and motivations - embrace this**

## Weekly Feedback

* Coordinate weekly feedback (typically Friday Afternoon) to ensure we are tracking:
  * Accomplishments
  * Status of current projects
  * Goals for future week
* Provide HONEST feedback on the week and overall efforts of students
* Ask for feedback, as a supervisor, to improve the student's internship

## Task Management

* Meet with students as needed (often 2-4 times a week) to ensure they understand the task they are working
* Provide guidance on the tasks of intended outcome
* Provide priority to what they should focus
* Enable individual thinking versus instructions so they think outside the box

## Student Feedback Coordination

* All feedback is confirmed with Doug before official to ensure we are on the same page as to expectations and efforts
* This is a requirement of Vanderbilt University Internship program
