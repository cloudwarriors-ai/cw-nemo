# General CSD Onboarding

## Onboarding / Orientation

* Conduct & Support Kindra with initial interview with the potential Hire before the member goes into a Technical Interview
* Set-up meeting with New Hire for their start day at **0930 EST**
  * Intended for this time to make sure they can attend the Daily DevOps Meeting at 1030 EST
  * Kindra, Doug, Chad, Trent invited for brief introduction if they would like to attend (optional)
* Meet with New Hire to introduce them to CW and provide an introduction, orientation, basic information and expectations moving forward

## Application Orientation

* Conduct a BASIC walkthrough of applications for they can test
* Sometimes this is done as a checklist in their onboarding package
* **Careful always in this process to not imply issues so the New Hire can find them naturally through using the application**

## Application Testing

* New Hire is expected to follow onboarding checklist testing guidance (provides step by step guidance on how to test)
* This allows New Hire to familiarize themselves with CW applications overall to better understand its purpose and functionality
* This provides an orientation to the application and the GitHub Repository to ensure they can effectively work on the application moving forward as a developer
* **This process shows A LOT about their character, work ethic and motivations - embrace this**

## Initial Task Management

* Meet with New Hire as needed (often 2-4 times a week) to ensure they understand the task they are working
* Provide guidance on the tasks of intended outcome
* Provide priority to what they should focus
* Enable individual thinking versus instructions so they think outside the box

## 'Graduation' Out of QA

* Goal is for the New Hire to orientate to CW and naturally transition from CSD QA to CSD Development
* Typically this occurs within the first 30 days
